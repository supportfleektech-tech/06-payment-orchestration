# Go-to-Market & Revenue

## Offer
Start with one outcome, not a feature list.

## Pricing
Use a simple Kenya-first ladder:
- Starter: low fixed monthly price with strict limits.
- Growth: higher usage, automation and reporting.
- Business: multi-user workflows and integrations.
- Enterprise: negotiated implementation/support.

Final pricing should be based on customer interviews and infrastructure/API costs.

## Sales motion
- Paid pilot with a defined outcome.
- Onboarding/migration fee where data setup is material.
- Monthly subscription.
- Annual discount only after retention is demonstrated.
- Referral/channel partner program after the direct sales motion works.

## Validation target
Before adding major scope, secure paid design partners and measure activation, weekly usage, retention, support burden and gross margin.
