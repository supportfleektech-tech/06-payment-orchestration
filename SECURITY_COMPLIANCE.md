# Security, Privacy & Compliance

## Baseline
- Data inventory and processing map.
- Controller/processor role assessment.
- Privacy notice and lawful-basis documentation where applicable.
- Access controls and least privilege.
- Secure secrets management.
- Encryption in transit and at rest where appropriate.
- Security logging and incident response.
- Vendor/subprocessor register.
- Data subject request process.
- Backup and disaster recovery.

## Kenya-specific gate
Assess ODPC registration/compliance requirements before processing personal data at production scale. Where the product is regulated or handles sensitive sector data, obtain sector-specific counsel.

## Financial integrations
Do not represent the product as a bank, PSP, remittance provider, lender or custodian of funds unless the business has the required licence/partner arrangement. Use licensed partners while the product is in the software/orchestration layer.

## Healthcare
For health products, require a separate clinical safety, data protection and regulatory workstream. No production patient data should be introduced before those controls are approved.
